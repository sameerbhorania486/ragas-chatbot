COMPANY LEAVE POLICY

1. Employees receive 12 casual leaves every year.
2. Employees receive 15 earned leaves every year.
3. Employees must inform their manager before taking leave.
4. Sick leave requires a medical certificate if the leave exceeds three consecutive days.
5. The company working hours are 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM.
6. Saturday and Sunday are weekly holidays.
7. Employees must maintain professional behavior at the workplace.
8. Salary is credited on the last working day of every month.
